# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14+ (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS (logical properties for RTL/LTR), Framer Motion, `next-themes` (Dark Obsidian `#09090B` & Light Studio Daylight `#F8FAFC`), and Lucide React icons.

## Users

Aspiring video editors, content creators, and freelance video professionals in both Arabic-speaking (MENA) and global English-speaking markets striving to build portfolio authority and land high-paying clients.

## Product Purpose

A high-conversion, ultra-modern SaaS-style landing page selling a comprehensive Notion Roadmap: *"From Zero to First Video Editing Client"* (covering Premiere Pro, After Effects, AI editing acceleration, and client acquisition).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Bilingual Bidirectionality (AR RTL & EN LTR)**: Seamless navbar toggle, complete logical CSS mirroring, tailored high-craft typography for both languages.
- **Dual Studio Theme**: "Editing Suite Obsidian" (`#09090B`) with glowing neon accents and "Studio Daylight" (`#F8FAFC`) with crisp contrast and warm diffused shadows.
- **Interactive Timeline Scrubber**: Pure front-end interactive timeline mockup where dragging the playhead highlights the 4 curriculum stages in real time.
- **Asymmetric Bento Grid**: Interactive Notion checklist preview, animated tech stack SVG beam (Pr + Ae + AI), and Client Blueprint visual card.
- **The Editors' Wall**: Dual-row infinite horizontal marquee of audio waveform/marker testimonials with hover-to-pause and verified result badges.
- **The Pricing Vault & FAQ**: Centerpiece pricing card with animated border beam, complete value stack, sleek lead capture / checkout intent modal, and accessible FAQ accordion.
- **Phase 1 to Phase 2 Architectural Readiness**: Modular event telemetry and checkout service abstractions allowing Phase 2 backend, payments, and admin dashboards to plug in without UI rewrites.
